In 6 months from the kick off of this course your drones will compete against each other in 3 categories:

Hover stability
The ability to hover in a single spot without drifting
Controlled 3d movement
Autonomous movement following a predefined course
An obstacle course
A timed human controlled lap around a drone obstacle course

The rules are as follows:

Your drones must have exactly 4 motors (quadrotor) 
They must fly in FPV mode
They must be built and integrated by the team
They must be safe to operate indoors under supervision

You may:
look at open-source designs
read build logs
study commercial drones

You may not:
directly copy CAD files
reuse PCB layouts
assemble kits

Everything must be:
Designed
Ordered
Assembled
Debugged
